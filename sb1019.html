What Is SB 1019?

Senate Bill 1019 is bipartisan legislation introduced by Senator Lisa Boscola (D-Northampton) and Senator Elder Vogel (R-Beaver) that creates preferred status for net metering on specific site types through 2050.

Net metering allows solar system owners to receive full retail-rate credits for excess electricity sent back to the grid — making solar significantly more valuable than selling at wholesale rates.

The bill is designed to:

  • Steer solar incentives to underused land (brownfields, minelands, warehouses)
  • Protect prime farmland from utility-scale solar sprawl
  • Address PUC concerns about cost-shifting from merchant generators
  • Create economic opportunity in energy transition communities

Which Sites Qualify?

Under SB 1019, these site types receive priority access to full retail-rate net metering credits:

Site Type Why It Qualifies
Warehouse Rooftops Underutilized space, high capacity potential
Brownfields Remediation + revenue opportunity
Abandoned Minelands Economic revitalization for coal communities
Parking Garage Canopies Dual-use infrastructure, shade + power
Capped Landfills Low-impact land use, limited alternatives
School Buildings Community benefit, educational value
Sites Near Closed Coal Plants Energy community transition support

Why This Matters for SE PA

Lancaster and Berks counties are experiencing a warehouse construction boom — millions of square feet of new rooftop capacity coming online annually.

Chester and Montgomery counties are part of the Sustainable Energy Partnership (SEP), committed to 100% renewable energy by 2035.

SB 1019 creates a clear path for these regions to deploy solar at scale, generate revenue for property owners, and supply virtual offsets to businesses with sustainability commitments.

The timing aligns with:

  • Record industrial investment ($419M in Lancaster, $381M in Berks in 2024)
  • 46 SE PA municipalities committed to 100% clean energy goals
  • 30% federal Investment Tax Credit available through 2032
  • Growing corporate demand for local renewable energy credits
