Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same as RECs (Renewable Energy Certificates)?
Similar concept, but more local. Standard RECs often come from wind farms in Texas or solar in Arizona. Our virtual offsets are sourced exclusively from SE Pennsylvania — the same grid you're connected to. This makes your sustainability claims more credible and keeps economic benefits in the region.
Can I really claim 100% renewable energy?
Yes. The offset matches your usage with equivalent local solar generation. For ESG reporting, B Corp certification, and customer communications, you can truthfully say your business runs on renewable energy. We provide documentation for verification.
What happens if the solar site generates less than expected?
We over-allocate capacity to account for weather variability. Your contracted offset amount is guaranteed regardless of production fluctuations at any individual site.
What's the commitment length?
Typically 1-3 year terms with the option to extend. Longer commitments often come with better rates. We can structure terms based on your budget planning cycle.
How does pricing work?
You pay a monthly rate per kWh of offset. This is typically competitive with or below standard utility rates, with the added benefit of price stability. We'll provide specific pricing based on your usage and the available capacity in your area.
