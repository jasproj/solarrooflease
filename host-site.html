Frequently Asked Questions

What does "preferred status" under SB 1019 actually mean?
It means your solar system gets locked into net metering at full retail electricity rates through 2050 — unlike standard commercial systems that utilities are trying to reclassify to lower wholesale rates. This protection is only available to specific site types listed in the bill.
What's the minimum roof size you work with?
Generally 20,000+ square feet makes a project economically viable, but it depends on roof condition, shading, and local utility rates. Our assessment will determine if your specific property qualifies.
Does my roof need to be in perfect condition?
Not perfect, but it should have 15+ years of useful life remaining. If your roof needs replacement soon, we can coordinate with roofing contractors — sometimes bundling solar with a new roof creates additional tax benefits.
Who handles maintenance?
Powerstream, our installation partner, provides 25-year performance monitoring and maintenance. Solar panels require minimal upkeep — mainly just occasional cleaning if debris accumulates.
What if I don't own the building?
Property managers and long-term lessees can participate with property owner consent. We'll help structure the agreement so all parties benefit.
